NOTICE INVITING TENDER
Construction of staff quarters and allied water supply works

Schedule of materials to be supplied by the contractor:

1. TMT reinforcement bars Fe 500D grade, 8 mm to 25 mm dia, conforming to IS 1786, quantity 45 MT
2. Ordinary Portland Cement 43 grade in 50 kg bags, ISI marked, 1800 bags
3. Coarse aggregate 20 mm nominal size and river sand for concrete, as per relevant IS
4. Structural steel angles and channels for roof trusses, hot rolled, conforming to IS 2062
5. Interlocking concrete paver blocks 80 mm thick, M30 grade, for the approach road
- Galvanized MS pipes medium class, 25 mm and 40 mm nominal bore, for internal plumbing
- UPVC pipes for soil and waste discharge, 110 mm dia, with all fittings
- Cast iron sluice valves 150 mm size with hand wheel for the pumping main
- Ductile iron pressure pipes DN 300, K9 class, for the external water supply main
* PVC insulated copper house wiring cable 1.5 sq mm and 2.5 sq mm FR grade, as per IS 694 : 2010
* Distribution transformer 250 kVA outdoor type, oil immersed, for the colony feeder
* Earthing of the substation shall be carried out as per IS 3043 latest version
The contractor shall supply safety helmets and safety shoes for all site workers.
Drinking water supplied at site shall be tested against IS 10500 before use.
Note: IS 8112 referred in the earlier estimate shall be read as superseded.
