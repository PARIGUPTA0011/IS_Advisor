How it works (offline, no LLM):
  1. The specification is split into line items; procurement boilerplate and citations are stripped.
  2. Hybrid retrieval over 23,341 current, latest-edition standards: keyword (BM25) and a local embedding model that also reads each standard's clause 1 scope, fused by rank.
  3. The knowledge graph expands every candidate: its normative references (clause 2), what it replaced, and whether each referenced standard is still current.
  4. Standards the tender cites are checked: a withdrawn one is flagged and its current replacement shown.
